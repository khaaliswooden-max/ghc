\section{zk-Halal Attestation}
\label{sec:zkhalal}

\subsection{Statement}

The prover is a producer in possession of (i) a provenance DAG $G$,
(ii) per-edge process-trust weights $w$, and (iii) the per-source
compliance labels $\ell$. The verifier is any consumer or regulator.
The relation we prove in zero knowledge is
\[
  \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{halal}} = \bigl\{\,
     (x, \omega) :
        x = \mathsf{Comm}(G, w, \ell) \;\wedge\;
        \HCF(\omega) \succeq \halal
  \,\bigr\}
\]
where $\omega = (G, w, \ell)$ is the witness, $\mathsf{Comm}$ is a
binding hash commitment (Poseidon over BLS12-381), and $\HCF$ is
encoded as an arithmetic circuit by composing the constituent
operations of $G$.

\subsection{Constructions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Native (off-chain) verifier}: Groth16
        \cite{groth16} over BLS12-381, implemented in
        \texttt{core/ghc-zk} on top of arkworks
        \cite{arkworks}. Trusted-setup is per-circuit; the
        circuit is fixed once the spec is frozen.
  \item \textbf{On-chain verifier}: a Circom port
        \cite{circom} of the same circuit, with a Solidity
        verifier deployed under \texttt{integrations/dlt}.
  \item \textbf{Universal-setup variant}: PLONK \cite{plonk} for
        deployments where per-circuit ceremonies are infeasible.
  \item \textbf{Transparent variant}: a STARK port for
        post-quantum settings; planned for v0.2.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Security}

Soundness reduces to the discrete-log assumption on BLS12-381 for
Groth16, and to the AGM + KZG \cite{kzg} assumptions for PLONK. Zero
knowledge is statistical against unbounded verifiers when the
trusted-setup parameters are honestly generated. Privacy of suppliers
and recipes is captured formally by an indistinguishability game in
\Cref{sec:eval}.
