\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Halal traceability.}
Tieman's foundational analyses of halal supply-chain management
\cite{tieman} and the empirical literature on halal integrity in
\emph{Food Control} \cite{ali_foodcontrol} establish the operational
vocabulary GHC builds on. Recent surveys of blockchain-based halal
traceability \cite{khan2020} document a proliferation of platform-specific
proposals; GHC differs in providing an authority-parametric mathematical
substrate rather than a single-stack platform.

\paragraph{Provenance and traceability standards.}
W3C PROV-O \cite{provo} provides the canonical RDF vocabulary for
provenance; GS1 EPCIS~2.0 \cite{epcis2} provides the standard event
schema for supply-chain visibility; GDST extends EPCIS to seafood. GHC
adopts EPCIS~2.0 as a baseline and contributes a halal-specific extension
profile (\Cref{sec:impl}).

\paragraph{Compositional semantics.}
The categorical perspective on compositional systems is developed in
\cite{maclane,fongspivak,spivak2012}. Recent work on functorial supply-chain
modeling has established the viability of categorical methods for
industrial provenance; GHC instantiates this for compliance lattices.

\paragraph{Information theory of contamination.}
The treatment of contamination as a noisy-channel problem draws on
\cite{covertom} and on Bennett's thermodynamics of information
\cite{bennett82}. We are not aware of prior work that bounds
contamination survival in compliance terms via channel capacity.

\paragraph{Quantum statistical mechanics of mixed populations.}
The use of density matrices to represent classical mixtures with
quantum-style partial-trace semantics follows \cite{nielsenchuang,petz};
the application to provenance is, to our knowledge, novel.

\paragraph{Zero-knowledge attestations.}
Modern SNARK constructions \cite{groth16,plonk,halo2} now make
practical proving of complex predicates feasible in milliseconds. GHC's
zk-Halal circuits (\Cref{sec:zkhalal}) compile compliance-lattice
membership to arithmetic circuits suitable for these systems.
