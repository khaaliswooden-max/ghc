\section{zk-Halal Attestation}
\label{sec:zkhalal}

\subsection{Statement}

The prover is a producer in possession of (i) a provenance DAG $G$,
(ii) per-edge process-trust weights $w$, and (iii) the per-source
compliance labels $\ell$. The verifier is any consumer or regulator.
The relation we prove in zero knowledge is
\[
  \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{halal}} = \bigl\{\,
     (x, \omega) :
        x = \mathsf{Comm}(\omega) \;\wedge\;
        \HCF(\omega) \succeq \halal
  \,\bigr\}
\]
where $\omega = (G, w, \ell)$ is the witness and $\mathsf{Comm}$ is a
binding Poseidon-over-BLS12-381 commitment. As of v0.0.x of the
reference implementation (`core/ghc-zk`), $\mathsf{Comm}$ is
realized concretely as a Poseidon sponge with width 3 (rate 2,
capacity 1), $\alpha = 17$, 8 full rounds, and 29 partial rounds,
absorbing the salt followed by the per-step verdict field elements.
The native (off-circuit) and in-circuit Poseidon hashers are
byte-identical and gated by a CI-enforced equality test
(`native\_and\_circuit\_poseidon\_agree`); $\HCF$ is encoded as an
arithmetic circuit by composing the per-step domain check
$w_i (w_i - 1)(w_i - 2) = 0$ with the threshold check
($w_i = 0$ for \halal-only, $w_i (w_i - 1) = 0$ for $\leq \mashbuh$).

\subsection{Constructions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Native (off-chain) verifier}: Groth16
        \cite{groth16} over BLS12-381, implemented in
        \texttt{core/ghc-zk} on top of arkworks
        \cite{arkworks}. Trusted-setup is per-circuit; the
        circuit is fixed once the spec is frozen. Implemented as of
        v0.0.x; reference benchmarks in \Cref{sec:impl}.
  \item \textbf{EVM verifier}: a Circom \cite{circom} port of the
        same statement targeting BN254 (alt\_bn128), with a Solidity
        Groth16 verifier auto-generated by \texttt{snarkjs} and
        deployed under \texttt{integrations/dlt/evm}. Implemented as
        of v0.0.x; the assertion suite in
        \texttt{integrations/dlt/evm/scripts/test.sh} verifies that
        a halal-only witness produces a valid proof and that haram
        verdicts are rejected at witness generation. The off-chain
        and EVM pipelines prove the same statement on parallel
        curves; \Cref{sec:impl} describes the spec-level
        \texttt{ghc:scheme} tag that prevents cross-curve confusion.
  \item \textbf{Universal-setup variant}: PLONK \cite{plonk} for
        deployments where per-circuit ceremonies are infeasible.
        Planned for v0.1.
  \item \textbf{Transparent variant}: a STARK port for
        post-quantum settings; planned for v0.2.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Security}

Soundness reduces to the discrete-log assumption on BLS12-381 for
Groth16, and to the AGM + KZG \cite{kzg} assumptions for PLONK. Zero
knowledge is statistical against unbounded verifiers when the
trusted-setup parameters are honestly generated. Privacy of suppliers
and recipes is captured formally by an indistinguishability game in
\Cref{sec:eval}.
