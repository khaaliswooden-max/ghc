\section{Discussion and Future Work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Authority federation.}
GHC's compliance lattice is parametric in a Shariah authority. The
v0.1 spec supports federated operation by indexing $\Lat$ over an
authority set $\mathcal{A}$ with explicit dissent encoding. A future
extension will study the semantics of cross-authority appeals using
the topos-theoretic machinery of \cite{maclane}.

\paragraph{Privacy budget.}
zk-Halal is privacy-\emph{preserving} in the cryptographic sense, but
the publication of provenance-fingerprint spectra leaks coarse
structural information about supply chains. We sketch a
differentially-private extension and leave its formal analysis to
subsequent work.

\paragraph{Hardware roots of trust.}
The deterministic capacity bounds in \Cref{sec:capacity} assume honest
sensor calibration; integrating remote-attestation-backed sensors
(e.g.\ Intel TDX, AWS Nitro) into the witness format is a natural next
step.

\paragraph{Standardization track.}
The v0.1 spec is targeted at an OIC/SMIIC working-group submission,
with parallel engagement of GS1 for an EPCIS halal-extension profile
and W3C for a Verifiable-Credentials schema annex.
