\section{The Najis Density Matrix}
\label{sec:najis}

\subsection{Provenance Hilbert space (Phase B+)}

For a finite set $\mathcal{S}$ of source-batch identifiers we define the
provenance Hilbert space $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{C}^{|\mathcal{S}|}$ with
orthonormal basis $\{\,|s\rangle : s \in \mathcal{S}\,\}$. A pure
provenance state is a unit vector $|\psi\rangle \in \mathcal{H}$;
operationally, $|\langle s | \psi\rangle|^2$ is the (epistemic)
probability that the batch in question originated at source $s$.

\begin{definition}[Najis density matrix]
\label{def:rhon}
For a batch $B$ produced by combining sources $s_i$ with mass fractions
$w_i$ and per-source provenance witnesses $|\psi_i\rangle$,
\[
   \rhoN(B) \;=\; \sum_i w_i\, |\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|.
\]
Mixing is direct sum followed by partial trace over discarded
sub-systems; separation is the corresponding adjoint.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Scalar diagnostics — Phase B+]
\label{prop:diagnostics}
$\rhoN(B)$ admits the following scalar reductions, each computable in
$O(|\mathcal{S}|^3)$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item \textbf{Purity} $\Tr(\rhoN^2) \in (1/|\mathcal{S}|, 1]$:
        certification confidence.
  \item \textbf{Von Neumann entropy} $S(\rhoN) = -\Tr(\rhoN \log \rhoN)$:
        provenance uncertainty in bits.
  \item \textbf{Najis spectral radius} $\lambda_{\max}(\rhoN \cdot N)$
        for $N$ the haram-source projector: the dominant haram-mode of
        the batch.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\Cref{prop:diagnostics} requires mathlib's \texttt{InnerProductSpace}
machinery. The \emph{purity bound}
$1/n \leq \Tr(\rhoN^2) \leq 1$ on the diagonal-basis representation
(equivalent under the spectral theorem to the abstract Hermitian
form) is now mechanized in
\path{proofs-mathlib/GhcMathlib/Density.lean} via
\texttt{Density.Diag.purity\_le\_one} (Cauchy--Schwarz on
$w_i^2 \leq w_i$ for $w_i \in [0,1]$) and
\texttt{Density.Diag.one\_div\_n\_le\_purity}
(Chebyshev's sum inequality
$(\sum w_i)^2 \leq n \sum w_i^2$ from
\texttt{Mathlib.Algebra.Order.Chebyshev}).
Von Neumann entropy bounds remain on the Phase~C+ roadmap.

\subsection{Discrete kernel (mechanized)}

The diagonal classical case --- which covers the overwhelming majority
of operational compliance decisions --- is provable in pure Lean.
Model a batch as a list of $(s_i, w_i, v_i)$ contributions where
$v_i \in \Lat$ is the source's verdict. The mix verdict is the meet of
contributions; mixing is concatenation.

\begin{theorem}[Najis closure kernel; mechanized]
\label{thm:najis-kernel}
For any batches $b_1, b_2$ encoded as contribution lists, and any
verdict $v$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(N\arabic*)]
  \item \emph{Mixing compositionality:}
        $\mathrm{verdict}(b_1 \mathbin{+\!\!+} b_2) =
         \mathrm{verdict}(b_1) \sqcap \mathrm{verdict}(b_2)$.
  \item \emph{All-halal closure:} if every contribution in $b$ is
        $\halal$, then $\mathrm{verdict}(b) = \halal$.
  \item \emph{Haram contamination:} if any contribution in $b$ is
        $\haram$, then $\mathrm{verdict}(b) = \haram$.
  \item \emph{Uniformity:} if $b$ is nonempty and every contribution
        has verdict $v$, then $\mathrm{verdict}(b) = v$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof (kernel)]
By induction on contribution lists; mechanized in
\texttt{proofs/Ghc/Quan\-tum.lean} via
\texttt{Quantum.Batch.verdict\_mix}, \texttt{verdict\_all\_halal},
\texttt{verdict\_haram\_member}, \texttt{verdict\_uniform}.
\end{proof}

\Cref{thm:najis-kernel} is the diagonal restriction of
\Cref{prop:diagnostics}: when each $|\psi_i\rangle = |s_i\rangle$ is a
basis vector and $w_i$ are interpreted as classical mass fractions,
purity collapses to the indicator that all contributions agree, and
the meet-of-verdicts identity becomes literal.
