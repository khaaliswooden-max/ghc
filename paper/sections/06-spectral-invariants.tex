\section{Spectral Provenance Invariants}
\label{sec:spectral}

\subsection{The provenance DAG}

The history of a batch is a directed acyclic graph $G = (V, E, w)$ with
$V$ the set of all upstream batches and operations, $E$ the
``derives-from'' relation, and $w \colon E \to (0,1]$ the per-edge
process-trust weight (computed from auditor reliability + sensor
calibration).

\subsection{The relabel/rename adversary}

\begin{definition}[Adversary class]
\label{def:adversary}
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rl}}$ is the class of polynomial-time adversaries
that may permute vertex labels and rename edges (e.g.\ recertify
batches, substitute supplier identifiers) but may not alter the graph's
combinatorial structure or edge weights.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Spectral fingerprint (Phase B+)}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral fingerprint --- Phase B+ statement]
\label{thm:spectral}
Let $L_G = D_G^{-1/2}(D_G - W_G) D_G^{-1/2}$ be the normalized
weighted Laplacian \cite{chung} of the provenance DAG. Then
$\spec(L_G)$ is invariant under every adversary in
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rl}}$, and two graphs with the same multiset of
eigenvalues are indistinguishable to such adversaries up to
isospectrality.
\end{theorem}

\Cref{thm:spectral} reduces to the invariance of the
\emph{characteristic polynomial} under simultaneous row/column
permutation: a permuted matrix is similar to the original via a
permutation matrix, hence has the same characteristic polynomial and
therefore the same eigenvalue multiset. The polynomial-invariance
form is now mechanized in
\path{proofs-mathlib/GhcMathlib/Spectral.lean} via
\texttt{Spectral.charpoly\_relabel}, with corresponding
trace and determinant invariance theorems
(\texttt{Spectral.trace\_relabel}, \texttt{Spectral.det\_relabel}).
The full eigenvalue-multiset extraction (over $\mathbb{R}$ for
symmetric Laplacians via the spectral theorem) remains on the Phase~C+
roadmap.

\subsection{Combinatorial kernel (mechanized)}

The operationally-decisive consequence of \Cref{thm:spectral} ---
``relabeling alone cannot alter the structural fingerprint of a
provenance graph'' --- has a combinatorial refinement provable in pure
Lean: the multiset of edge weights, the count of edges with any given
weight, and the total edge weight are all preserved under arbitrary
vertex relabelings.

\begin{theorem}[Combinatorial fingerprint kernel; mechanized]
\label{thm:spectral-kernel}
Let $g$ be a directed weighted graph encoded as a list of edges
$(s, t, w)$ over $\mathbb{N}$, and let $\sigma \colon \mathbb{N} \to
\mathbb{N}$ be any vertex-relabeling. Define the relabeled graph
$\sigma(g) := \{(\sigma s, \sigma t, w) : (s,t,w) \in g\}$. Then
\begin{enumerate}[label=(S\arabic*)]
  \item \emph{Weight sequence:} the multiset of edge weights is
        preserved: $\mathrm{weightSeq}(\sigma(g)) = \mathrm{weightSeq}(g)$.
  \item \emph{Count invariance:} for every weight $w \in \mathbb{N}$,
        $\#\{e \in \sigma(g) : e.\mathrm{weight} = w\} = \#\{e \in g :
        e.\mathrm{weight} = w\}$.
  \item \emph{Total invariance:} $\sum_{e \in \sigma(g)} e.\mathrm{weight}
        = \sum_{e \in g} e.\mathrm{weight}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof (kernel)]
By induction on the edge list; mechanized in
\texttt{proofs/Ghc/Spectral.lean} via
\texttt{Spectral.Graph.weightSeq\_relabel}, \texttt{countWeight\_relabel},
and \texttt{totalWeight\_relabel}.
\end{proof}

In production, GHC stores the top-$k$ eigenvalues of $L_G$ as the
``provenance fingerprint'' alongside each attestation; the kernel
\Cref{thm:spectral-kernel} guarantees that the simpler weight-multiset
fingerprint already detects every relabeling-only attack, providing a
defense-in-depth that is constant-time to verify.
