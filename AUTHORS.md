# Authors

GHC is the work of "The GHC Authors" — the maintainers and
contributors listed below, plus the open-source ecosystems we build
on. Order is alphabetical; presence here does not imply endorsement
of any specific cryptographic, juristic, or political position
expressed elsewhere.

For *current* maintainer roles (review responsibilities, release
authority), see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Maintainers

## Contributors

* The GHC Authors (collective placeholder for v0.1.0; individual
  contributors are credited in the commit log and `CHANGELOG.md`
  as they land changes).

To add yourself to this file, open a PR that:

* appends your name (or pseudonym) and a contact handle to the
  list,
* references at least one merged commit you authored,
* includes a DCO sign-off (`git commit -s`).
