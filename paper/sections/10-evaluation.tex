\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

We evaluate GHC along four axes: theorem coverage, runtime performance,
ML accuracy, and end-to-end demonstrability. Detailed numbers will
populate this section as Phases C--E complete; the structure below is
the locked evaluation plan referenced from the spec.

\subsection{Theorem coverage}
We report the fraction of theorems in this paper that typecheck under
\texttt{lake build}, broken down by section. Target: $100\%$ at v0.1.

\subsection{ZK performance}
For zk-Halal Groth16 over BLS12-381 we report
proving time, verification time, proof size, and circuit constraint
count, parameterized by provenance-DAG size $|V| \in \{10^2, 10^3,
10^4\}$.

\subsection{ML accuracy}

The v0.0.x ML pipelines (\path{services/ghc_ml}) are evaluated on
deterministic synthetic data so the numbers reported here are
reproducible from a clean checkout via \texttt{pytest}.

\paragraph{Ingredient classifier.}
\texttt{IngredientClassifier} combines a corpus arm (curated
haram-keyword + E-number lattice) with a character n-gram MLP
fallback. On the synthetic dataset
\path{ghc_ml.data.synthetic.synthetic_ingredients} (seed=0,
$N{=}400$, 80/20 split), the neural arm converges to validation
accuracy $\geq 0.90$ in 30 epochs of CPU training. The corpus arm
guarantees correctness on known tokens (pork, lard, alcohol,
E120, E322, ...) by construction. Real labeled-image evaluation
on multilingual labels (Arabic, Malay, Urdu, Indonesian, English)
is Phase E+ work that will plug a LayoutLMv3 image encoder ahead
of the n-gram MLP.

\paragraph{Risk GNN.}
\texttt{SupplyChainRiskGNN} achieves validation MAE $< 0.20$ and
PR-AUC $> 0.30$ on \path{ghc_ml.data.synthetic.synthetic_dags}
(seed=0, $N{=}80$, 80/20 split, 80 epochs). Real-data GDST
evaluation is queued for Phase E+ once a redistribution-friendly
trace corpus is licensed.

\subsection{End-to-end demos}
Three cross-domain demos exercise the full pipeline:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Beef sample:} farm $\to$ abattoir $\to$ processor
        $\to$ retailer with EPCIS events, zk-Halal VC, Fabric anchoring.
  \item \textbf{Pharmaceutical excipient:} gelatin source-to-API audit
        with $\rhoN$ purity scoring.
  \item \textbf{Sukuk contract:} AAOIFI-aligned attestation chain with
        federated multi-authority lattice.
\end{enumerate}
