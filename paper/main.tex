\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{Global Halal Compliance (GHC)}, a cross-domain
protocol unifying halal compliance across food, pharmaceuticals,
cosmetics, Islamic finance, and logistics under a single mathematical
substrate. The protocol is grounded in six novel, machine-checked
contributions: (1) the \emph{Halal-Closure Functor} mapping a symmetric
monoidal category of supply-chain processes to a complete distributive
compliance lattice; (2) the \emph{Contamination Channel Capacity}
$\Ch$, a closed-form information-theoretic upper bound on
haram-signal survival across processing chains; (3) the \emph{Najis
Density Matrix} $\rhoN$ borrowed from open-system quantum
statistical mechanics; (4) \emph{spectral provenance invariants} on
provenance DAGs that resist relabel/rename adversaries; (5) a
\emph{zk-Halal} attestation scheme reconciling open traceability with
supplier privacy; and (6) a \emph{Compliance Convergence Theorem}
quantifying audit sufficiency. The kernel theorems and the
mathlib-dependent extensions (density-matrix purity bounds,
characteristic-polynomial invariance, Chebyshev / Chernoff audit
concentration, exponential channel survival under no-reinvention)
are all machine-checked in Lean~4 \cite{lean4} with zero
\texttt{sorry}s and CI enforcement. The reference implementation
ships zk-Halal Groth16 over BLS12-381 (off-chain) and over BN254
(EVM-deployable Solidity verifier), six fixture-backed certifier
adapters (JAKIM, MUI/BPJPH, HFA, ESMA, SFDA, SMIIC), bidirectional
EPCIS~2.0 \cite{epcis2} translation, a Hyperledger Fabric chaincode,
and two trainable PyTorch ML pipelines (ingredient classifier and
supply-chain risk GNN). The protocol is specified as a versioned
open standard with JSON Schema and is frozen at v0.1.0.
\end{abstract}
