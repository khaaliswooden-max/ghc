\section{Lean 4 Formalization Index}
\label{app:lean}

The GHC kernel is formalized in pure Lean 4 (no \texttt{mathlib}
dependency) under \path{proofs/}. Every numbered theorem in the body
of this paper corresponds either to a fully-proven kernel theorem
(table below) or to a Phase B+ extension (real-analysis-heavy claims
that will be mechanized once \texttt{mathlib} is integrated).

\paragraph{CI gate.} The repository's CI runs
\texttt{lake build} on every push and additionally greps for
\verb|sorry| in the kernel source tree; either failure breaks the
build. The kernel currently contains zero \texttt{sorry}s and zero
unsafe \texttt{axiom}s, and compiles in under twenty seconds on a
stock Ubuntu runner with no \texttt{mathlib} cache.

\paragraph{Phase C+ track.} Statements that still need substantial
mathlib infrastructure: the full mutual-information formulation of
$\Ch$ (mathlib does not yet ship a mutual-information development);
Hoeffding's lemma (sub-Gaussian MGF bound for bounded random
variables); Azuma-Hoeffding for martingales; and the spectral-theorem
reduction connecting the diagonal-basis density-matrix purity bound
to abstract Hermitian density matrices.

\paragraph{Smoke checks.} \path{proofs/Ghc/Smoke.lean} enumerates
every kernel theorem with \verb|#check| and provides concrete
\verb|decide|-checked examples for HCF and plurality, ensuring no
theorem can be silently weakened or removed without breaking the
build.
