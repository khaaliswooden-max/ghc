\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

Every numerical result and figure in this paper is regenerated from the
notebooks under \path{notebooks/} via papermill, with datasets pinned
by content hash via DVC \cite{dvc}. The top-level recipe is
\texttt{make repro}, which:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item resolves DVC pointers in \path{datasets/};
  \item executes notebooks in dependency order;
  \item regenerates figures into \path{paper/figures/};
  \item rebuilds the paper PDF.
\end{enumerate}
A successful CI run on the \texttt{main} branch is the canonical
reproducibility certificate; per-release builds are archived to
Zenodo.
