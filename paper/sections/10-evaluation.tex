\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

We evaluate GHC along four axes: theorem coverage, runtime performance,
ML accuracy, and end-to-end demonstrability. Detailed numbers will
populate this section as Phases C--E complete; the structure below is
the locked evaluation plan referenced from the spec.

\subsection{Theorem coverage}
We report the fraction of theorems in this paper that typecheck under
\texttt{lake build}, broken down by section. Target: $100\%$ at v0.1.

\subsection{ZK performance}
For zk-Halal Groth16 over BLS12-381 we report
proving time, verification time, proof size, and circuit constraint
count, parameterized by provenance-DAG size $|V| \in \{10^2, 10^3,
10^4\}$.

\subsection{ML accuracy}
Ingredient-OCR F1 on a held-out test set of multilingual product labels
(Arabic, Malay, Urdu, Indonesian, English); GNN supply-chain risk
PR-AUC on a synthetic-plus-GDST evaluation set.

\subsection{End-to-end demos}
Three cross-domain demos exercise the full pipeline:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Beef sample:} farm $\to$ abattoir $\to$ processor
        $\to$ retailer with EPCIS events, zk-Halal VC, Fabric anchoring.
  \item \textbf{Pharmaceutical excipient:} gelatin source-to-API audit
        with $\rhoN$ purity scoring.
  \item \textbf{Sukuk contract:} AAOIFI-aligned attestation chain with
        federated multi-authority lattice.
\end{enumerate}
